# Privacy Policy for MoneyMa

Last updated: March 21, 2026

MoneyMa ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal finance application that helps users record, review, back up, and synchronize personal financial information across supported devices.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use MoneyMa, we may collect and process the following categories of information:

### A. Account Information

- Email address
- User account ID
- Authentication-related information required to sign in and manage your account

### B. Financial Information

- Income and expense records
- Transaction amounts
- Categories and budget entries
- Dates
- Optional descriptions or notes you choose to enter

### C. App Preferences

- Language settings
- Theme settings
- Other app configuration preferences stored for your experience

### D. Backup and Sync Data

When cloud backup or synchronization is enabled, the financial and preference data associated with your account may be uploaded to and stored using Supabase services so it can be backed up and synchronized across devices.

### E. Crash Logs and Diagnostics

We may collect crash logs, error reports, and technical diagnostics to:

- Monitor app stability
- Investigate bugs
- Improve performance and reliability

This information may include app version, device model, operating system version, timestamps, technical identifiers, and error traces. We do not use crash reporting data for advertising.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

- Creating and managing user accounts
- Authenticating users and maintaining secure sign-in sessions
- Storing and displaying financial records
- Generating summaries, reports, and statistics
- Supporting backup, restore, and cross-device synchronization
- Remembering user preferences
- Detecting, investigating, and fixing crashes or technical issues
- Protecting the security and integrity of the app and related systems

## 3. How Information Is Stored

MoneyMa may store information in two places depending on the feature being used:

- Locally on your device, including app storage or app container data
- On secure cloud systems used for account services, cloud backup, and synchronization

Locally stored data may still remain on your device even when cloud sync is enabled.

## 4. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal or financial information.

We may share information only in the following limited situations:

- With service providers that help us operate authentication, cloud storage, synchronization, hosting, or crash reporting
- When you explicitly use export, backup, restore, or sharing features
- When required by law, regulation, legal process, or valid government request
- To protect the rights, safety, security, or property of users, MoneyMa, or the public

MoneyMa uses Supabase for account authentication and cloud data synchronization. If other third-party infrastructure providers are used, such as a crash reporting provider, they may process data on our behalf under their own security and processing terms.

## 5. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

MoneyMa may use crash reporting or diagnostics tools to understand app failures and improve app quality.

Crash and diagnostic data may include:

- Crash stack traces
- Device and operating system details
- App version and build number
- Performance-related metadata
- Technical identifiers associated with the app installation or reporting service

We use this information only for debugging, maintenance, reliability, and service improvement.

## 6. Cloud Backup and Synchronization

If you sign in and use cloud backup or sync features:

- Your financial records and related app data may be transmitted to Supabase-managed infrastructure used for MoneyMa account and sync services
- Data may be synchronized between devices associated with your account
- Data may be restored after reinstalling the app or signing in on another supported device

You are responsible for reviewing data on all devices connected to your account.

## 7. Login and Account System

When you create or use an account, we may process account-related data to:

- Register your account
- Sign you in securely
- Identify your synced data across devices
- Support account recovery, security, and service continuity

If you contact support, we may also use your account identifier or email address to help resolve account-related issues.

MoneyMa uses Supabase Auth to support sign-in, session handling, and account-linked data access.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and for legitimate business, security, and legal purposes.

Examples:

- Account information may be retained while your account remains active
- Cloud backup and synchronization data may be retained until you delete it, delete your account, or request deletion, subject to technical backup retention windows
- Crash logs and diagnostics may be retained for a limited period necessary for troubleshooting and service improvement

Retention periods may vary depending on operational, security, legal, or compliance needs.

## 9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information.

These may include:

- Secure transmission methods such as HTTPS/TLS
- Access controls
- Authentication safeguards
- Controlled infrastructure and service-provider access

However, no method of storage, transmission, or electronic processing is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. User Choices and Controls

Depending on the features available in your version of MoneyMa, you may be able to:

- Edit or delete records within the app
- Export your data
- Restore data from a backup
- Enable or disable certain backup or sync features
- Sign out of your account
- Request deletion of your account or associated cloud data
- Uninstall the app and remove local app data from your device

## 11. Children's Privacy

MoneyMa is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

## 12. International Data Transfers

If Supabase or any other cloud, hosting, authentication, or crash reporting services used by MoneyMa operate in different countries, your information may be processed or stored outside your country of residence.

Where applicable, we will rely on reasonable safeguards appropriate to the services used.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. If changes are material, we may also provide additional notice through the app, website, or other appropriate means.
